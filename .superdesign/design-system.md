# Table Talk
Personal foosball score tracker for friends. Main job: record goals quickly on a phone, revisit rivalry records on desktop. Shared edits without login. Rivalries home, rivalry H2H and match history, live game scorer, completed report.

A sports match journal, inspired by the bold numeric hierarchy and decisive controls of Kinetic Orange; use the restrained product palette below rather than its promotional landing-page treatment. No marketing hero images or pricing, no sidebar bloat.

Colors: navy #111d24 page; slate #20323c panels; off-white #edf2ef text; muted #93a4ac; player blue #83c8f0; player orange #f3b07e. Body system sans/Arial, display bold sans with tight tracking; tabular score figures. Spacing 4,8,12,16,24,32,48,64px. Rounded panels 20px, controls 10px, badges fully rounded. Hairline slate borders; subtle shadows only for overlay dialogs. No gradients.

Desktop centered 1120px max width, simple header with foosball mark and Table Talk wordmark, Rivalries link, connection status. Large main heading Your rivalries, concise supporting copy, New rivalry button. Pair cards with names/initials in blue and orange, large head-to-head score, completed games and streak, thin divided supporting facts. A quieter recent games list and short instructions area. Mobile cards single column. Live scoring must show both sides simultaneously with four >=48px rung buttons each.

Use compact plain language and sentence case, no filler. Brand is friendly competitive; personality through score typography and foosball table linework. No external images necessary. Respect reduced motion and native accessible dialogs. Default first to 10 win by two, finish manually. Own goal credits opponent. Timestamps minute:second.
